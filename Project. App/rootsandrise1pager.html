The Problem
A system built for a child that doesn't exist

Millions of children in Kenya's informal settlements enter and exit primary school without mastering basic reading, writing, or numeracy. This is not a child's failure — it is a systemic failure rooted in undertrained teachers, language barriers, overcrowded classrooms, hungry learners, and digitally excluded adults.

"The system was designed around an ideal learner. That learner is not the reality for millions of Kenyan children."

— Roots and Rise, Problem Statement
Root Causes
  • Teachers deployed without FLN-specific pedagogy
  • Language of instruction excludes the learner
  • No ECDE before Grade 1 for millions of children
  • Hunger reducing concentration and attendance
  • Home environment cannot reinforce school learning
  • Digital and financial exclusion of parents

Our Solution — Three Pillars
Pillar 1 — Teacher Professional Development
5-day FLN bootcamp, monthly refreshers, and quarterly classroom coaching co-designed with TaRL Africa, Uwezo Kenya, and PAL Network. Educators earn a certified Roots and Rise FLN credential.
Pillar 2 — Child-Centred Learning Sessions
Level-based grouping using TaRL diagnostics. Daily uji (porridge) for every child. Max 1:10 ratio. Storytelling, phonics, and numeracy games in Swahili or mother tongue.
Pillar 3 — Adult Literacy & Digital Inclusion
Evening and weekend classes for parents, vendors, and boda boda operators. Basic literacy, functional numeracy, M-PESA skills, and scam detection — taught in Swahili or mother tongue.
Expected Outcomes
Improved foundational literacy and numeracy in early grades
Stronger home-school connection through adult literacy
Certified, confident community educators retained in-community
Adults protected from financial scams and exploitation
