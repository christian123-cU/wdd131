Nairobi is the capital and largest city of Kenya, situated in the south-central part of the country at an elevation of 1,795 m (5,889 ft). It is the most populous city in East Africa and serves as a major financial and commercial hub for the region. Nairobi is home to the only national park located within a major capital city in the world — Nairobi National Park — making it uniquely rich in both urban culture and wildlife.
