The Crisis

A system built on assumptions, not realities

Millions of children in Kenya's informal settlements and rural areas enter and exit primary school without mastering basic reading, writing, and numeracy. This is not a child's failure — it is a systemic failure.

The numeracy proficiency rate among Grade 3 learners dropped from 68.8% in 2019 to 54.2% in 2022 — despite curriculum reforms and billions in government spending. Business as usual is failing.

Our Programme

Three interlocking pillars

We simultaneously address the learner, the teacher, the parent, and the home environment — because partial solutions produce partial results.

🏫
01

Teacher Professional Development

Structured, ongoing training for early grade and community teachers — co-designed with TaRL Africa, Uwezo Kenya, and the PAL Network. Every teacher earns a certified YouthRise FLN Educator credential.

  • 5-day intensive bootcamp
  • Monthly peer learning circles
  • Quarterly classroom coaching
👶
02

Child-Centred Learning Sessions

Level-based grouping using TaRL methodology, maximum 1:10 teacher-to-student ratio, daily porridge for every child — because a hungry child cannot learn.

  • Ability-based, not age-based groups
  • Daily uji (porridge) programme
  • ECDE gap-bridging for out-of-school children
👩‍👩‍👧
03

Adult Literacy & Digital Inclusion

A child who goes home to a parent who cannot read cannot be fully supported. Adults gain literacy, numeracy, and mobile money skills that protect them from exploitation.

  • Basic literacy & functional numeracy
  • M-PESA & digital safety skills
  • Evening & weekend sessions

Theory of Change

The root determines the fruit

If teachers are professionally equipped, and children learn at their own level in a language they understand, and they are nourished, and adults in the home are also growing in literacy and financial confidence — then foundational literacy and numeracy will improve, school readiness will increase, economic vulnerability will reduce, and an intergenerational cycle of exclusion will begin to break.
