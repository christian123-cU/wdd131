The Programme

A community-based,
multi-pronged intervention

YouthRise Changemakers operates through three interlocking pillars — because no single fix can solve a systemic failure.

Root Causes

Why the gap persists

The problem has not been solved because solving it requires reaching the people and places that are hardest to serve — in the right language, at the right level, with sustained commitment. No single actor has done all of that at once. That is exactly the gap YouthRise exists to fill.

Pillar 01

Teacher Professional Development

Teachers are the single most important school-level factor in learning outcomes. Without equipping them with the right tools, no curriculum reform succeeds.

YouthRise conducts structured, ongoing professional development training co-designed with and endorsed by TaRL Africa, Uwezo Kenya, and the PAL Network.

Pillar 02

Child-Centred Learning Sessions

Children cannot learn when they are hungry, grouped beyond their level, or taught in a language they do not understand. This pillar rebuilds the learning environment from the ground up.

🍵
Daily Porridge Programme

Every session begins with a warm cup of uji (porridge) for every child. This is not a bonus — it is a core programme component. Hunger directly reduces concentration, memory, and participation.

Pillar 03

Adult Literacy & Digital Inclusion

A child who goes home to a parent who cannot read cannot be fully supported. Adults in low-income communities also face daily exploitation due to numerical and digital illiteracy — from business theft to mobile money scams.
