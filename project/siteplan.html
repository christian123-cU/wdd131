Site Name

Proposed domain roots-and-rise.org

Why this name?

Roots speaks to the foundational literacy and numeracy skills that every child and adult must have before anything else can grow. Rise captures the upward momentum that follows — learners rising out of learning poverty, communities rising together.

The name is short, bilingual-friendly, and resonates across donor audiences, partner organisations such as PAL Network and RELI (Regional Education Learning Initiative), and the families of Mathare whom this programme directly serves.

Full programme name: Roots and Rise — Foundational Literacy & Numeracy Programme, implemented by YouthRise Changemakers.

Site Purpose

The Roots and Rise website is the first digital home for a community-based FLN programme operating in Mathare, one of Nairobi's largest informal settlements.

📣

Communicate Impact

Surface the FLN crisis in Kenya's informal settlements through data and human stories, helping visitors understand why foundational learning matters.

🤝

Attract Support

Provide a clear, credible entry point for funders, volunteer teachers, and partner organisations to engage with or contribute to the programme.

📚

Explain the Programme

Describe the three pillars — Teacher Training, Child Learning Sessions, and Adult Literacy — with enough clarity to inform and inspire action.

📬

Enable Contact

Offer a simple, accessible contact form and social links so any interested party can reach YouthRise Changemakers with minimal friction.

Planned Pages

  1. Home — Mission headline, programme overview, call to action
  2. The Problem — FLN crisis data and visuals for Kenya's informal settlements
  3. Our Programme — Three pillars explained with icons and summaries
  4. Get Involved — Ways to volunteer, donate, or partner
  5. Contact — Form, location, and social links

Scenarios

The following questions represent the site's target audiences — funders, potential volunteers, partner organisations, community members, and researchers. Answering these drives the site's content decisions.

Q1

What exactly is the Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN) crisis, and why does it matter in Mathare specifically?

Audience: Funder / new visitor

Q2

How does Roots and Rise teach children — what methods are used, how often do sessions run, and what results have been achieved so far?

Audience: Parent / community member

Q3

I'm a teacher or youth worker — how can I volunteer with the programme, and what training or commitment is required?

Audience: Potential volunteer teacher

Q4

Our organisation funds literacy initiatives in East Africa. What evidence does YouthRise Changemakers have of impact, and how can we partner?

Audience: Institutional donor / partner NGO

Q5

Is there an adult literacy component? My mother never learned to read — can she join the programme?

Audience: Community family member

Q6

How is Roots and Rise different from other literacy programmes already operating in Nairobi's informal settlements?

Audience: Researcher / journalist

Color Schema

The palette draws from the warm earth of Mathare's soil and the bright yellow of shared hope — grounded, human, and optimistic.

Sunrise Yellow

#F5C518

Primary accent — hero backgrounds, call-to-action buttons, highlight bars, logo mark. Evokes warmth, hope, and visibility.

Deep Earth

#111111

Primary text, navigation background, headings, footer. Provides strong contrast and a grounded, serious tone.

Rise Red

#A93226

Secondary accent — data callouts ("X% of children cannot read"), urgent statistics, section emphasis on "The Problem" page.

Warm Cream

#F0EAE0

Page background, card backgrounds, body text regions. Softer than pure white, easier on the eye in bright sunlight on mobile.

Stone

#3D3830

Primary body text on light backgrounds. Achieves 9.5:1 on cream and 12:1 on white — well above WCAG AA.

Accessibility note: All text/background combinations meet WCAG AA (4.5:1 minimum). Yellow (#F5C518) on Deep Earth (#111111) reaches 9.5:1. Stone (#3D3830) on Cream (#F0EAE0) reaches 9.5:1. All combinations validated.

Typography

Two Google Fonts are selected for legibility, character, and zero cost. A third monospace font is used sparingly for data and statistics.

Display & Headings · Playfair Display

We rise by lifting others.

Used for the hero headline, page titles (H1, H2), and the site name/logo wordmark. Playfair's editorial serif voice conveys seriousness and humanity — right for a programme that demands to be taken seriously.

Weights used: 700 Bold, 900 Black

Body & UI · DM Sans

Every child deserves the right to read. Foundational literacy is not a luxury — it is the foundation of everything.

Used for all body paragraphs, navigation links, button labels, card content, and form elements. DM Sans is a modern, low-contrast geometric sans designed for comfortable screen reading across sizes and weights.

Weights used: 300 Light, 400 Regular, 500 Medium

Data & Statistics · DM Mono

89% · 2.4M learners · Grade 3

Used sparingly for statistical callouts, counter numbers, and inline data labels. The monospace rhythm gives statistics a clinical, trustworthy feel — separating them visually from narrative text.

Weights used: 400 Regular, 500 Medium
