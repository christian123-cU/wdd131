The Unlikely Birth of Boardlords Chess Club: A Story of Passion and Potential

2026 Mashimoni, Mathare

It started with a founder who knew only a few moves, three curious learners, and a borrowed board. It's now 34 players strong — and ranked nationally.

In the heart of Mashimoni, a vibrant community within Mathare, a game of strategy and skill was ignited. Our founder, Mr. David Oduor, a university-educated enthusiast, brought back stories of chess — a game he'd learned only a few moves of — sparking curiosity and interest among locals. Despite his limited knowledge, Mr. David's passion was contagious, drawing in players like the now Lead Coach, James. Thus, Boardlords Chess Club was born — a testament to community-driven growth.

From humble beginnings with just three learners, the club has flourished into a thriving community of 34 dedicated players. Our progress has been remarkable, with consistent participation and impressive performances at regional and national levels.

In 2025, eight players represented us at nationals, with most ranking in the top 10 nationwide. However, financial constraints limit our reach. We rely on partners like ChallengeAid Africa to support our talented players. This year, with their help, six out of eight players scored four points and above at regionals — a clear signal of real, sustained potential.

"Chess isn't just for the privileged — it's for anyone with passion and drive."
