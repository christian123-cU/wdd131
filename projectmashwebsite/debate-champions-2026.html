From Mathare to the Podium: YouthRise Changemakers Redefine What's Possible

April 2026 Mathare School of Hope Cluster

Eight young debaters walked into a 24-school cluster competition. Four of them walked out ranked in the Top 5 orators — out of more than 2,000 students.

Out of 24 Schools of Hope across Nairobi's Mathare cluster, Mashimoni SOH showed up and stood out — fielding a formidable squad of four incisive juniors and four eloquent seniors. While our articulate juniors battled their way to the quarterfinals, our senior team commanded the floor all the way to the finals and was crowned Best Debating Team 2026.

But it doesn't stop there. Among over 2,000 brilliant young minds across the 24 schools, all four Mashimoni seniors ranked in the Top 5 orators of the cluster:

1st
Sharlyn Stacy (Grade 10) — Outstanding Debater, razor-sharp rebuttals
2nd
Phiona (Grade 10) — Best Speaker, poise and precision
4th
Allan (Form 3) — commanding rhetoric
5th
Emmaculate (Grade 10) — persuasive flair

This isn't just a win — it's a declaration. Talent, intellect, and world-class eloquence are thriving in the heart of Mathare. Watch this space: by Grade 11, these rising advocates won't just top the charts — they'll set a new standard.

"Four seats in the Top 5, out of 2,000 competitors. That's not luck. That's what happens when a community decides its young people deserve a stage."
