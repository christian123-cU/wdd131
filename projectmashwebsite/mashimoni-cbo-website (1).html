Our Journey

Who We Are

Mashimoni CBO was born out of a simple observation: too many children in Mathare were missing school due to financial challenges, unsafe spaces, and limited opportunities.

What began as a small library where children could read and study has grown into a lifeline for families — combining education, mentorship, and extracurricular activities that transform lives.

With the incredible support of ChallengeAid, Education in a Suitcase, and our growing network of partners, learners have been able to thrive — building confidence through digital lessons, mentorship, and hands-on learning tools.

Today, we proudly serve hundreds of children and families, proving that when a community invests in its young people — transformation follows.

Our Core Purpose

Why We Exist

In Mathare, dreams often collide with reality — a reality where poverty, hunger, and lack of opportunity make it hard for children to stay in school or believe in a better tomorrow.

Mashimoni CBO exists to change that story.

We believe every child deserves a chance to succeed — whether through education, sports, or creative expression. When a child learns, plays, or discovers their talent, something powerful happens: confidence grows, potential unfolds, and a brighter future begins.

From Small Beginnings

Our History

Mashimoni CBO started with a simple vision: create a safe space for children to read, learn, and dream. A small library — a handful of books, a few children, one big belief that education changes everything.

Over the years, we've grown into a vibrant community hub, offering literacy programs, mentorship, extracurricular activities, sports programs, and digital learning opportunities that transform lives in Mathare.

From weekly shopping distributions and sanitary towels for girls, to the Girls and Boys Club, Mathare Got Talent, and interactive Zoom lessons — every program was born from a real need in our community.

Today, we proudly serve hundreds of children and families, proving that when a community invests in its young people — and when kind partners walk beside them — transformation follows.

Our Foundation

Mission & Vision

Our Mission
Building Strong Children Through Education
Our mission is to support children in Mathare with the tools they need to succeed in school and in life. We focus on education, mentorship, wellbeing, and family involvement — because a child does not grow in isolation.

By working closely with parents, teachers, and community partners, we create safe spaces where children are supported, guided, and encouraged to stay in school. Our work helps learners build confidence, discipline, and direction — growing into responsible young people with purpose, self-belief, and a clear sense of possibility.
01
Our Vision
Raising Youth to Lead Generational Change
We envision a future where children from underserved communities grow into confident, capable young leaders who shape their own paths — and the paths of those who come after them.

Our aim is to build a community-led education model that can be adopted beyond Mathare — one that strengthens learning, character, and responsibility. By grounding our work in local ownership and shared values, we seek long-term impact that reaches families, communities, and future generations.
02
